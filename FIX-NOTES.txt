Easyway Learn Clean Rebuild v1.0

Rebuilt OCR pipeline instead of applying another small patch to the previous OCR selector.
Added image normalization, multi-pass script-aware OCR, deterministic candidate scoring, and explicit OCR diagnostics.
OCR routes are stateless and do not require Authorization.
Reading/Speaking UI retains continuous recognition with explicit Start/Stop/Complete semantics.
Missing words are rendered without underline; only recognized expected words receive the correct-word underline class.
The package was syntax-checked, ZIP-tested, and exercised against local English/Hindi/mixed OCR samples.
