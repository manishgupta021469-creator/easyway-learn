Easyway Learn V68 — OCR definitive PDF/image fix

1. PDF upload now tries pdftotext first. Text-based PDFs are extracted directly instead of being rendered/OCRed.
2. Scanned PDFs fall back to page-by-page Tesseract OCR with fast PSM 6 and PSM 11 passes.
3. Long PDFs no longer run three OCR passes per page, reducing Render timeouts.
4. OCR remains session-independent; saving page data remains authenticated.
5. JSON/request limits increased for ordinary textbook PDFs sent as base64.
6. Hindi/English Tesseract and Poppler remain installed by Dockerfile.
7. Service worker cache bumped to V68.

Validation: node syntax checks, direct unauthenticated image OCR, embedded-text PDF extraction, scanned-PDF OCR and ZIP integrity.
