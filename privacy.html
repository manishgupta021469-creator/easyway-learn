Easyway Learn — Privacy Information

Version 0.45 • Last updated 24 September 2026

What the app handles

Easyway Learn is designed for textbook learning, reading qualification, speaking assessment, scores, history, progress and content sharing. A permanent Student ID links a student’s learning records across classes/academic years.

Learning and assessment data

The app may store student profile information, learning content, reading/speaking attempts, scores, high scores, history, progress, usage summaries, shares and feedback reports. Historical assessment snapshots are intended to remain preserved when common content is corrected.

Camera and microphone

Microphone access is used for speech recognition/assessment when the student starts a supported recording flow. Camera access is optional for anti-cheating evidence. The product specification requires trigger-based evidence screenshots and does not permit continuous video storage.

Security

Passwords are stored using a salted password-hashing method. Server sessions use expiring tokens, requests are rate-limited, production deployments are intended to use HTTPS, and security headers are enabled by the backend.

Sharing

When a student shares learning content, the receiving student must accept the share before full learning features are available. Scores, attempts, history and progress remain student-specific.

Support

Support is user-initiated. The application does not send automatic monthly performance emails.

Important: This page describes the product’s implemented privacy controls and the requirements recorded in the Easyway Learn master specification. A public launch should use a final legal/privacy review and publish the operator’s real contact details and data-retention/deletion procedures.
